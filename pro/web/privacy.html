Who we are

[Business or owner name and address]. Contact: [email address]. We decide how your information is used (we are the "controller").

The short version

  • You can use the free taster without an account. Then your answers stay on your own device.
  • If you sign in, we save your progress and your work to your account so it follows you between devices.
  • When you ask for feedback on your writing, your task and your writing are sent to an AI service to be marked. We do not keep what you send, and we do not send your name or email with it.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We do not sell your information.
  • You can download everything we hold, or delete your account, from the Account page.

What we hold, and why

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to contact you about your account. (Contract / legitimate interests.)
  • Your saved work and progress: your answers, plans, drafts, which missions you finished and when. To give you the service on every device. (Contract.)
  • Your plan: whether you have Plus, until when, and a payment reference from our payment partner. We never see your card details. (Contract; legal duty to keep payment records.)
  • How many AI feedbacks you have used this month, to apply the monthly allowance and control costs. (Contract / legitimate interests.)
  • Technical logs: the time, page and result of each request, with no writing and no email addresses, kept for [30] days to keep the service working and safe. (Legitimate interests.)

The one cookie

When you sign in we set one cookie that keeps you signed in. It is needed for the service to work, so it does not need a consent banner. It is not used for tracking.
